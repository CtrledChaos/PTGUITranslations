# Instagram Saves, Batch 3: Full Compilation of 277 Posts

Compiled 2026-10-21 from the 277 Instagram links you sent in one dump. The posts were published between 2026-04-28 and 2026-10-20. Each post has its own full entry in Part 4.

## What each entry contains (your 6 criteria)
1. **Audio:** a timestamped account of everything said, with exact quotes of the key lines, and a translation where the speech wasn't English. Background music is named where it could be identified; lyrics are never transcribed.
2. **On-screen content, page by page:** every distinct page or screen of the reel or carousel, in order, with its text as displayed. The OCR was hand-corrected against the frames.
3. **Post description:** the caption only (no comments). Short captions are quoted in full. Long ones keep their opening verbatim, and every remaining fact, link, step and hashtag is listed (see "Copyright and privacy" below).
4. **Everything named or linked:** a table of every URL, tool, repo, @handle, ticker, price, number and claim, with where it appeared and what it really is after verification.
5. **What's being promoted / the funnel decoded:** what "comment X", "link in bio" or "DM me" actually leads to, who is behind it, and the price where one was found.
6. **Verification and insights:**
   - Repos were checked with git: license, last commit and README.
   - Products and claims were web-searched.
   - Trading claims were tested for realism.
   - Connections to other posts are noted.

Each entry also has a header with the account, the date, the post type and length, a category and a one-line verdict.

## Verdict scale
| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LEGIT & FREE** | Real, and free or open source |
| **LEGIT – PAID** / **LEGIT** | Real, but costs money, or is real information without a free tool behind it |
| **PARTLY TRUE** | Real core, but with overclaims, missing caveats or stale information |
| **MISLEADING** | The main claim is wrong or misattributed |
| **SCAM-RISK** | Unrealistic returns, a fake "verified" record, or pressure funnels |
| **PIRACY-ToS RISK** | A "free premium" scheme, cracked tools, or account-sharing that breaks a site's terms |
| **NO SUBSTANCE** | Nothing usable, or the post couldn't be retrieved |

## How it was done
- **Fetching:** media came from an Instagram mirror (InstaFix) and from yt-dlp, because Instagram rate-limited direct requests (HTTP 429). Full captions came from Instagram itself wherever it allowed them.
- **Pages:** ffmpeg detected every scene change, so each page change in a slideshow reel became its own full-resolution frame. Frames were also taken at 2 per second to catch missed changes.
- **Text and speech:**
  - OCR used RapidOCR, as a draft only. Every page was then read by eye and the OCR corrected.
  - Speech was transcribed with faster-whisper ("small"), with machine translation where needed.
- **Review:** a reviewer viewed every page image and slide, read the transcript, verified every named item, and decoded the funnel.
- **Trims and audits:** two compliance trims and a final audit kept quoted text short and removed secrets and private data. No downloaded code, and no code shown in a post, was ever executed.

## Limits you should know about
- **Captions:** Instagram released full captions only slowly (roughly one every 5 minutes, with long rate-limit pauses). Where an entry says *"Caption source: possibly truncated (mirror)"*, the caption came from the mirror and may be cut off at about 250 characters. The video, its pages and its audio were still fully reviewed. A background job is still fetching the remaining full captions, and those entries will be updated in a follow-up commit.
- **Cover-only entries:** a few reels could only be retrieved as a cover image. Their entries say "reviewed from cover image only". The same job is retrying their videos.
- **Unretrievable:** a handful of posts could not be retrieved at all; they are listed in Part 3.
- **Unverifiable items:** star counts, follower counts, and anything behind a "comment X for the DM" wall (the DM content itself) could not be verified unless it was public elsewhere.

## Copyright and privacy
- **What is kept in full:** every fact, name, link, number, rule, step and price.
- **What is shortened:** long verbatim captions, long monologues and long code blocks are given as the opening plus fact bullets, timestamped notes with short exact quotes, or a short code excerpt. Song lyrics are never reproduced.
- **What is redacted:** API keys that creators leaked on screen, and private third-party data such as client names and fees.
